# Theme Store listing — QuillCommerce

## Name
QuillCommerce

## Tagline (one line)
Quiet, fast, and honest — a storefront that works for every shopper,
with or without JavaScript.

## Description
QuillCommerce is built for merchants who sell real gear to people who
use it. Three brand colors become a complete, accessible palette; the
type scale, corner style, and motion all follow from a handful of
settings — no designer required. Three ready-made styles (Headwater,
Driftwood, Slate) give three different stores out of the box.

Every flow is progressively enhanced: shoppers on any device, any
connection, or any assistive technology can browse, filter, and buy.
The slide-in cart, quick view, side-by-side compare, and live variant
syncing layer on top of native forms that keep working when everything
else is stripped away.

**Selling features**
- Variant-synced product page: gallery with full-size zoom, pills or
  dropdown picker with taxonomy color swatches, price/stock states
  with a low-stock threshold and inventory-capped quantities,
  deep-linkable variants and selling plans, unit prices, quantity
  rules, volume pricing, and automatic pre-order labeling
- Product badges — automatic Sale plus custom badges from tags — with
  hover-swap second images and swatch dots on every card
- Local pickup availability per location, updated as the shopper picks
  variants
- Slide-in cart drawer mirrored perfectly with the cart page — edits
  apply instantly on both (switchable to page-only)
- Quick view and one-click quick add from every product card
- Side-by-side product compare (up to four) and a private
  recently-viewed strip — no apps, no tracking
- Related products plus Search & Discovery "Pairs well with" pairings
- Storefront-building sections: slideshow, hero, promo banner, promo
  tiles, testimonials, countdown timer, FAQ (with rich-results
  structured data), image hotspots, quick order list, rich text, image
  with text, multi-column, featured collection, catalog navigator,
  newsletter, contact form
- Mega menu or dropdown navigation, sticky header, gift-card recipient
  sending, sticky purchase bar, collapsible product-info rows, trust
  badges, optional age verifier
- App blocks on the product page; full classic customer-accounts suite
- Five languages bundled: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian;
  right-to-left locales supported

**Performance & accessibility**
- ~87 KB of first-party CSS+JS total (before Shopify's CDN
  minification), zero external dependencies
- Lighthouse accessibility 100 on home, product, and collection:
  keyboard-complete, screen-reader announcements, 44px targets,
  reduced-motion aware, meaning never by color alone
- Works in pre-2023 browsers via server-computed color fallbacks

## Categories / industries
Sports & recreation · Outdoor · Hobbies — catalogs of any size that
value speed and clarity; quick order list covers light B2B.
