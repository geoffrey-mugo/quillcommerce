## Rules
- Prefer one collection/author for the editorial series; note if any asset breaks
  that and why.
- Only licenses that permit commercial demo use and redistribution in a theme
  submission. Record the exact license (e.g. Pexels License, Unsplash License, or
  a commission agreement).
- No asset enters the store until its row is complete and **Approved**.
