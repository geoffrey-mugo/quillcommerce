## About (`about`)
**Tailwater Supply Co.**

Tailwater Supply Co. is a river outfitter. We sell the equipment and the clothing
we use ourselves — rods, reels, lines, flies, and the layers that keep a long day
on the water comfortable — and nothing we would not fish with.

We started behind a fly-tying bench and a single vise. The catalog has grown, but
the rule has not: everything here earns its place by being used first, on cold
mornings and in bad weather, before it is offered to anyone else.

We build for repair, not replacement. Shells are re-taped, rods are re-wrapped,
and reels are serviced rather than retired. Gear that lasts is the least wasteful
gear there is.

### Our guides {#guides}
Our staff guide the water they sell for. When you ask what works, the answer comes
from someone who fished it this week, not from a spec sheet.

## Returns & repairs (`returns-repairs`)
**Returns.** Unused gear can be returned within **30 days** for a full refund. A
prepaid return label is included with every order; start a return from your order
email or the [Contact] page.

**Waders, boots and apparel** must be unworn with tags attached. Flies, leaders,
tippet and gift cards are final sale for hygiene and value reasons.

**Repairs.** We repair what we sell. Shells get a **free first-season re-tape**;
rods are covered by the maker's warranty and we handle the claim for you; reels
are serviced at cost. Send it in with a note and we will tell you what it needs
before we touch it.

## Size guide (`size-guide`)
### Apparel (chest, inches)
| Size | XS | S | M | L | XL | XXL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chest | 34–36 | 36–38 | 38–40 | 40–43 | 43–46 | 46–49 |

Layers are cut to wear over a base and under a shell. If you are between sizes and
plan to layer, size up.

### Rod line weight — what to fish
| Line weight | Best for |
|---|---|
| 3–4 wt | Small creeks, delicate dries, close range |
| 5 wt | The all-rounder — most trout rivers, dries to light streamers |
| 6 wt | Bigger water and wind, heavier nymph rigs |
| 8 wt | Bass, big streamers, and windy days |

### Reel size
Match the reel size range to the rod: **4/5** balances a 4–5 wt, **5/6** a 5–6 wt,
**7/8** a 7–8 wt. The reel should hold your line plus backing and balance the rod
in hand.
