## About (`about`)
**Tailwater Supply Co.**

Tailwater Supply Co. is a fly shop in Bend, Oregon. We sell rods, reels, flies,
leaders and the tackle that goes with them, plus a small apparel capsule cut for
long days on the water. We stock what we fish, and not much else.

We started behind a tying bench and a single vise. The catalog has grown, but the
rule has not: gear earns its place by holding up on cold mornings and in bad
weather before it reaches the shelf.

We build for repair, not replacement. Shells are re-taped, rods are covered by
their maker's warranty and we handle the claim for you, and reels are serviced
rather than retired. Gear that lasts is the least wasteful gear there is.

## Returns & repairs (`returns-repairs`)
**Returns.** Unused gear can be returned within **30 days** for a full refund. A
prepaid return label is included with every order; start a return from your order
email or the Contact page. Apparel must be unworn with tags attached. Flies,
leaders and gift cards are final sale.

**Repairs.** We repair what we sell. The Storm Wading Jacket gets a **free
first-season re-tape**; rods are covered by the maker's warranty and we file the
claim for you; reels are serviced at cost. Send it in with a note and we tell you
what it needs before we touch it.

### Choosing a rod line weight
| Line weight | Best for |
|---|---|
| 3–4 wt | Small creeks, delicate dries, close range |
| 5 wt | The all-rounder — most trout rivers |
| 6 wt | Bigger water and wind, heavier nymph rigs |
| 8 wt | Bass, big streamers, windy days |

### Matching a reel
Match the reel size to the rod: **4/5** balances a 4–5 wt, **5/6** a 5–6 wt, and
**7/8** a 7–8 wt. The reel should balance the rod in hand.

## FAQ (`faq`)
**Do you price-match?** No — we price fairly and stand behind repairs instead.

**Can you help me pick a first setup?** Yes. A 5-weight Riffle rod, a Riffle reel,
a 9ft 5X tapered leader, and the Dry Fly Assortment covers most trout water. Ask
us and we tailor it to where you fish.

**Are your flies barbless?** The Dry Fly Assortment is; others are available
barbless on request.

**Which leader should I use?** Match tippet to the fly, not the rod — see the
guide in Field Notes. Our Tapered Leader 3-Pack comes in 4X, 5X and 6X.

**Do you ship internationally?** To Canada; see Shipping.
